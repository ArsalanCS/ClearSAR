§ 01 / CAPABILITIES

Purpose-built for operators who can’t wait for clear skies.

SAR · Optical · BLIP-2
24/7 · all-weather
/01

See through clouds & dark

Radar penetrates weather and night cycles. Our fine-tuned latent diffusion converts the raw backscatter into an optical image a human — or a downstream CV model — can read.

/02

Physically grounded

We do not hallucinate infrastructure. Diffusion priors are anchored to the SAR input so generated scenes reflect what the sensor actually observed.

/03

Automatic captions

BLIP-2 vision-language model produces a 10-50 word natural-language summary of every translated scene. No remote-sensing PhD required.

/04

Real-time latency

End-to-end under 40 seconds per 256² scene on commodity GPU hardware. Batch pipelines push thousands of scenes overnight.

/05

Analyst-first UX

Side-by-side slider, region annotation, confidence meters, PDF report export. Built for mission briefings, not ML dashboards.

/06

API & on-prem

REST + Python SDK for pipelines. On-prem and air-gapped deploys for customers who need the weights on their side of the fence.
